01

Brand essence

Nudgebar puts your next meeting in your menu bar and nudges you before you miss it. Quiet by default, loud only when it matters.

→ Bedrock

Four corner dots form the structural identity. Every state — idle, active, urgent — preserves the four anchor points and changes the center.

→ Calm

Quiet by default. The menu bar shows a soft bell, the popover sleeps. Loudness is reserved for fired alerts — every other surface stays restrained.

→ Honest

Plain spoken, terse. "Coffee with Daniel · in 5 min" rather than "🍵 Reminder: Coffee Meeting in 5 Minutes!"

→ Native

Sits inside macOS, not on top of it. Real status item height, real vibrancy, real NSPopover behavior. Brand expresses through palette and typography; chrome stays macOS.

03

Logo system · Bold States Ring

Three states reflect what Nudgebar is doing right now. The four corner dots persist as structural anchors; the center and halos change. This is the only mark that earns brand recognition at favicon size.

Idle
No event imminent. Four anchors only. Status item is quiet, popover sleeping.
Active
Events tracked. The center dot lights up. Menu bar shows the next title + countdown.
Urgent
Alert window is up. Cardinals hold the structure; the center swells and gains two halos. The brand stays anchored — only the alert grows.

App icon · Urgent state

macOS app icon uses the Urgent state because Launchpad / Dock thumbnails appear when the user is choosing to engage. Rendered in a macOS rounded square (36 px corner radius at 160 px) with a brand-ink gradient ground.

08

Tone of voice

Plain, terse, kind. Nudgebar speaks the way a useful colleague would: short sentences, relevant facts, no flourish. Never demands; never apologizes for showing up.

DO
"Coffee with Daniel · in 5 min"

Title + relative time. The user reads "what" and "when" in under a second. No verbs, no padding.

DON'T
"🍵 Reminder: Your Coffee Meeting Starts in 5 Minutes!"

Emoji adds noise. "Reminder:" is redundant — the alert is the reminder. Title case and exclamation feel theatrical.

DO
"All clear"

Empty states are calm and complete. Two words, one declaration. The user can move on.

DON'T
"You have no upcoming events at this time."

Bureaucratic. Mentions the user, mentions time, mentions absence. Three negatives where one positive will do.

DO
"3 events starting soon"

Headline structure: count, subject, when. Honest about scale without dressing it up.

DON'T
"📅 Multiple Events Are About To Start! 🚨"

Alarm-clock pretending to be friendly. Capital letters and emoji can't make the same point as accurate plain text.
