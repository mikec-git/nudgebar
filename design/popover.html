Why mix B2 and D1?

B2 answers "what do I do right now?" with the next 1-2 actionable events in hero treatment. D1 answers "how does the rest of my day look?" with spatial honesty and folded quiet periods.

Combining them gives the popover two distinct jobs in clearly separated zones — no information competes for the same visual weight. The hero pair handles the next 5 minutes; the timeline handles the next 24 hours.

Layering rules

  • Hero zone: first 1 or 2 events whose fire-time is within the lead window OR within 60 min. If only 1, show a single full-width hero.
  • Timeline zone: everything else through end of tomorrow, capped at 23 (since 2 are in the hero zone, total stays at 25).
  • Quiet folds: gaps ≥ 90 min collapse to a "N hours quiet" divider.
  • Day boundary: "Tomorrow" divider is always shown even if today has no later events.
  • Empty hero zone: when nothing imminent, hero becomes a single calm card showing the next event with relative time ("in 2 h 14 min").
