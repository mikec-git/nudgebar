A · Outlined hairline

1.6 px stroke, rounded caps and joins. Reads cleanly at small sizes (16-24 px) without losing identity. Native macOS pattern — sits next to SF Symbols without clashing.

B · Outlined + 4-dot accent

Stroke base plus four small dots at brand-significant points. Picks up the Bold States Ring identity. Risk: dots can crowd at 16 px in dense surfaces (status item, popover rows).

C · Filled glyphs

Solid fills, heavier visual weight. iOS-flavored. Distances the system from macOS native chrome where it lives. Best reserved for app icon, not UI surfaces.
