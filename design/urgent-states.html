Why V2 (concentric pulse) is recommended

  • Semantic split: the four cardinal dots remain the brand's structural anchors (they don't change between active and urgent). Only the center carries the "urgency" — that matches what's actually happening (the alert fires from the center of the system).
  • Implies motion without animating: three concentric rings emanating outward read as "pulsing" even in a static asset. Useful because the app icon and a printed brand book share the same mark.
  • Holds at 16 px: rings collapse to a single soft halo at status-item size, but the four cardinals + center stay legible. V0's overlapping circle halos blur into one mushy ring at the same scale.
  • No Target / Olympics resemblance: the open rings + isolated cardinals don't echo any well-known mark.
  • Easy to animate later: in the real app, the inner ring can scale-up + fade-out while a new ring spawns in the center — natural pulsing tied to the alert sound's 5 s loop.
